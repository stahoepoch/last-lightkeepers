# Dock opening scream recordings

`opening-dock-scream-A.wav`, `-B.wav` and `-C.wav` are excerpts from
[Man screaming.wav by Archeos](https://freesound.org/people/Archeos/sounds/325548/),
licensed [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).
The excerpts use the public HQ preview, trimmed at 1.03, 4.60, and 14.04
seconds, high-pass filtered at 110 Hz, and exported as mono PCM.
