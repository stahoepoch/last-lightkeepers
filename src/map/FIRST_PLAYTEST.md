# First-playtest script — Flooded Mall

Run this only after the Studio MCP connection is on and the CollectionService
tags are applied. The goal is not balance; it is to learn whether the mall reads
in the first three minutes.

## Setup

- Start solo first, then repeat with two players.
- Begin at `Extraction_LoadingDock`.
- Keep one run permanently dark; in the next, use the lantern to pursue a relic.
- Capture a screenshot from the upper gallery looking into the Rain Garden.

## Pass conditions

| Moment | What a first-time player should understand | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Spawn | "I am in an abandoned two-storey mall." | They find the concourse without instructions. |
| First sightline | "That glowing planted court is where I should go." | They name or move toward the Rain Garden. |
| Relay | "This is a safe place to bank, but it exposes me." | They choose when to approach it. |
| Exit choice | "Loading Dock is safe; Freight Lift is deeper." | They can identify both routes on one run. |
| Light choice | "Light gets me something darkness does not." | They explain that rare loot is the reason to light up. |

## Fail signals and map-only fixes

| Failure | First fix to try |
| --- | --- |
| Rain Garden is not obvious | Raise the amber beacon and open the Food Court overlook. |
| Players get lost upstairs | Add one teal truss or rail break framing each ramp. |
| Relay feels like a free safe room | Place exposed loot sightlines into the garden; do not add walls. |
| Freight Lift is never found | Put its blue reflection visible from the east ramp. |
| Water looks like impassable death | Keep it shallow and retain a dry edge path. |

## Do not tune yet

Do not change Storm Charge rates, enemy damage, payout amounts, or lantern costs
from this test. Those are Claude/Chris-owned system decisions. Report player
movement, confusion, and the exact moment the crew chooses to turn on light.
