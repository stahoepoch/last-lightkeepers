# Last Lightkeepers — current pitch

Status: **working pitch, 2026-11-14.** This is a way to describe the current
game, not a promise that every story detail is settled. The old co-op raid
pitch is preserved at `docs/archive/PITCH-RAID-2026-10.md`.

## One line

**Alone in a storm-damaged mall, a young Lightkeeper uses one lantern to find
his family, outthink the machines still running the place, and find a way
home.**

## What you play

Explore real-scale stores and service spaces in first person. Listen for
machines doing their jobs, hide behind counters, choose a route around them,
and run when a plan goes wrong. Use the lantern to read what the dark hides and
to operate parts of the building, knowing its light can give you away. Find
people and evidence, solve physical problems, and push toward the next place
in a connected, changing Valley.

The tone is psychological horror with discovery and hope. This is a solo story
with RPG progression, stealth and environmental puzzles. It is not a co-op
extraction raid or a Roblox game.

## The proof behind the pitch

The first playable crossing must let someone move through both floors of
Boswell's, use cover and the lantern, survive a machine, learn why Sayers
matters, and reach it. See `GAME-THESIS.md` and `ROADMAP.md`. Until that works
in Unreal, this is direction, not a claim that the current build delivers it.
